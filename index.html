How it's calculated
Wwater = W1 × M2 − M1100 − M2

W1 is the starting grain weight, M1 the starting moisture and M2 the target moisture (both % wet basis). Tempering keeps the dry matter constant while the added water toughens the bran and softens the endosperm for cleaner roller-mill separation.

Practical tempering tips

1. Apply evenly

Spray the water while stirring or tumbling the grain so it doesn't pool, which can cause mold.

2. Seal while resting

Keep the grain in a tightly closed food-grade bucket or drum so moisture can't escape.

3. Room temperature

Temper at 65–75°F (18–24°C). Cold grain absorbs water slowly; warm grain risks sprouting.
